Onboarding

Live in four weeks

No professional-services engagement, no six-month integration project. A structured onboarding that gets telemetry flowing in week one and full detection coverage by week four.

Start onboarding See the platform

Week 1 — Discovery & connection

Asset inventory, crown-jewel identification, and telemetry sources connected via agent or API. Log flow verified end to end.

Week 2 — Baseline & tuning

Behavioural baselines established for identity, network and endpoint. Initial rule set deployed in monitor-only mode.

Week 3 — Runbooks & authority

Containment runbooks agreed per asset class, escalation paths defined, and the limits of our authority signed off in writing.

Week 4 — Go live & validation

Detections move to active. We run a purple-team exercise against your estate to prove coverage before you rely on it.
