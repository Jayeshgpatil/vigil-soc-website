Compliance and data handling

A security vendor asking for access to your estate owes you specifics. This page states what we hold, where it lives, who can reach it, and what we commit to — including the parts that are still in progress.

Certifications

Where we stand

Status is stated plainly. An audit in progress is described as in progress, because a logo on a website is not the same as a report you can read.

SOC 2 Type II Certified

Annual audit covering security, availability and confidentiality. The current report and bridge letter are available to customers and prospects under NDA — request them through the contact form.

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Certified

Information security management system covering the SOC platform, the analyst operation and supporting corporate systems. Statement of Applicability available on request.

ISO/IEC 27017 & 27018 In progress

Cloud-specific controls and personal data in cloud processing. Gap assessment complete; certification audit scheduled. We will publish the date it completes, not before.

Cyber Essentials Plus Certified

UK government-backed scheme with hands-on technical verification, renewed annually. Required by most UK public sector procurement.

Data

What we collect, and what we do not

Collected
  • Security telemetry: process execution, network connections, authentication events, file integrity changes.
  • Asset inventory: hostnames, addresses, operating system, agent version, owner.
  • Detection output: alerts, risk scores, the evidence behind each score.
  • Console activity: who viewed, assigned, escalated or exported what, and when.
Never collected
  • Passwords or credential material from monitored endpoints.
  • Keystrokes, screenshots, webcam or microphone input.
  • Document contents, mailbox contents or personal files.
  • Browsing history unrelated to a detection.

Monitoring is read-only. The agent does not disable host security controls or modify system security settings.

Handling
Encryption in transitTLS 1.3, HSTS with preload. Agent connections pin the service certificate.
Encryption at restAES-256 on all storage volumes and backups.
Credential storagePasswords: scrypt with per-user salts. API keys, reset tokens and recovery codes: SHA-256, never reversible. No credential can be displayed again after issue.
Telemetry retentionRaw events 30 days, alerts and incidents 13 months, audit log 7 years. Longer terms available by contract.
Data residencyUK and EU regions. Data does not leave the region you select, including for backup.
Tenant isolationOwnership is applied as a query filter rather than a post-fetch check, so there is no code path where a missing check exposes another tenant's data.
DeletionOn termination, telemetry is destroyed within 30 days and backups age out within 90. A certificate of destruction is issued on request.

GDPR and UK GDPR

VGIL acts as a processor for customer telemetry and as a controller for our own account records.

  • A Data Processing Addendum with the Standard Contractual Clauses is available and forms part of every contract.
  • A Data Protection Impact Assessment template for deploying endpoint monitoring is provided during onboarding.
  • Subject access, rectification and erasure requests are actioned within 30 days.
  • Breach notification to affected customers within 72 hours of confirmation, with the facts we have at the time rather than a holding statement.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you have found a security issue in this service, tell us at security@vgil.io. We acknowledge within one business day and will keep you updated until it is resolved.

Please do not run automated scanners against production, access or modify data that is not yours, or degrade service for other users. Testing that stays within those bounds will not result in legal action from us. Our machine-readable policy is at /.well-known/security.txt (RFC 9116).
