Platform

How a signal becomes a decision

The VGIL platform is a detection pipeline, not a dashboard. Everything below runs on the same engine producing the alerts you can watch on the live console.

1 · Ingest

Agents and API connectors pull endpoint, identity, network, cloud and SaaS telemetry into a normalised schema. Roughly 1.4 million raw events per detection reach the pipeline.

2 · Correlate

Events are stitched into entity timelines — this user, this host, this session — so a lateral movement chain reads as one story rather than forty disconnected log lines.

3 · Detect & score

ATT&CK-mapped rules and behavioural models fire. Each detection is scored on rule severity, asset criticality and indicator confidence — the same formula the API returns as risk.

4 · Respond

Above your threshold, an analyst is paged and pre-authorised containment runs. Everything is written to an immutable action log with attribution and a documented rollback.

Risk scoring

Why the same alert means different things

A generic severity label is close to useless. The same detection on a domain controller and on a designer's laptop demand completely different responses, so VGIL scores every alert against the asset it landed on.

risk-scoring — engine.js
risk = severityWeight + assetCriticality + indicatorConfidence
— severity critical 45 · high 34 · medium 20 · low 10
— asset critical 30 · high 22 · medium 12 · low 5
— confidence (0–100) × 0.25
