How it works

From signal to containment in four moves

Collect everything that matters

Endpoint, identity, network, cloud control plane, SaaS audit logs and email gateway telemetry stream into one normalised timeline. Deployment is agent-and-API based and typically completes inside two weeks.

Detect with rules that know your estate

Behavioural analytics and a curated ATT&CK-mapped rule set score each event against asset criticality and live threat intelligence. Tuning runs monthly against your own false-positive data, not a generic baseline.

Triage with a human, fast

Anything scoring above your agreed threshold reaches a named analyst in under a minute. You get the verdict, the evidence and the recommended action — not a raw alert forwarded to your inbox.

Contain inside the runbook

Where you have pre-authorised it, we act: isolate the host, revoke the session, block the destination, reset the credential. Every action is logged, attributed and reversible, with the rollback documented before it is taken.
