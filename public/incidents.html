Response process

What happens when we declare an incident

The same six phases run every time, whether it is a single phished mailbox or an estate-wide ransomware event. Only the pace changes.

PHASE 1

Detect & declare

A detection crosses the escalation threshold. An analyst validates it, assigns a severity and priority, and starts the clock. P1 incidents page the on-call lead and your nominated contact within four minutes.

PHASE 2

Scope

Before touching anything we establish blast radius: which identities, which hosts, which data. Containing half an intrusion is worse than containing none — it tells the adversary you are watching.

PHASE 3

Contain

Isolation, session revocation, credential resets and edge blocks execute inside the pre-authorised runbook. Anything outside it needs a named approver on the bridge before it runs.

PHASE 4

Eradicate

Persistence mechanisms removed, implants hashed and hunted estate-wide, the initial access vector closed. We do not restore service until the way in is shut.

PHASE 5

Recover

Systems returned to service from validated clean state, with heightened monitoring on the affected segment for a minimum of fourteen days.

PHASE 6

Review

A blameless write-up inside five working days: timeline, root cause, what detection missed, what tuning changed, and the evidence pack your regulator, insurer or board will ask for.
